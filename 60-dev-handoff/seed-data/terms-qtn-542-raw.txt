B. Consultation & Supervision of Erection, Installation & Commissioning
of Rolling Mill
A. Scope of Services.
1. Preparing Plant Lay-out
2. Detail Engineering of Mechanical, Electrical and civil work involved in the project.
3. To provide technical assistance & supervision for successful commissioning of the
projects.
4. We shall depute a team of suitable persons for erection and Installation of the Steel Plant.
5. Supply of foundation plans with load data for all the equipment including requirement of
embedded parts, trenches for cabling & piping etc. to enable customer arrange for design
wand construction of civil works.
B. Facilities to implement project.
The client will provide following -
1. All local manpower skilled and unskilled workers required for erection.
2. All civil work will be done by the client as per our drg. & instruction of our Engineer.
3. All tools, tackles and material handling equipment like cranes etc. raw materials,
consumables, Oil & Lubricants, Water, compressed air, power etc. will be provided by the
buyer at buyer’s cost to facilitate erection & commissioning.
4. All expenses Air Ticket, visa charges, insurance, lodging, Boarding, local conveyance
etc. shall be borne by the client.
5. You will cover all our personnel engaged at project site by suitable insurance policy
according to status of the person.
6. You have to pay actual Salary of all Persons coming on Site for Erection & Installation
7. You have to provide all Staff for Hot trial & Production of the Plant.
