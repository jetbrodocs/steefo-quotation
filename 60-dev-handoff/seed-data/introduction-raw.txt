INTRODUCTION
Steefo Engineering Corporation is an engineering company with the State of the art
manufacturing facility, specialized in the field of Rolling Mill & Billet Plant technology
and is fully committed and dedicated to the steel and related Industries.
Supply of various equipment and process technology for Rolling Mill & Billet Plant are
one of the core activities of Steefo Engineering and we design, manufacture and supply
Turn Key and tailor-made systems for the Steel Plant.
In the last 45 years Steefo Engineering has acquired the technology and Know How for
the entire production of quality steels by working with metallurgists and process experts
who have worked and gained valuable experience in the metal production industry
worldwide.
Steefo Engineering is able to design and built single units as well as whole production
plants for the Rolling Mill & Billet Plant from one hand ensuring single cast design of the
entire plant.
We have done Steel Plants of Reputed Groups on Turn Key basis in around 20 countries,
including India, Indonesia, Saudi Arabia, Jordan, Qatar, Angola, D.R of Congo, Ethiopia,
Mozambique, Kenya , Uganda; Zimbabwe, Mali, Nigeria, Ghana, Rwanda, Sri Lanka,
Bangladesh, Gabon, Sudan, Benin, Libya, UAE and Mauritius.
All our above projects are working satisfactorily due to good quality of finish material,
low cost of production, low maintenance and better after sales service.
